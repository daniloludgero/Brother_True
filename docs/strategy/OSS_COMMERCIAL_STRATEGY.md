## 1. Executive Thesis: Radical Transparency Compounds Trust

In AI systems engineering, wrapping a third-party API with prompt templates and marketing it as "proprietary artificial consciousness" destroys technical credibility on contact.

Following the proven **CryptographyBaSingSe** COSS playbook, **BrotherTrue** captures long-term value through **verifiable systems engineering and open-book scientific honesty**:
1. **100% Open-Source Core Engine & Contracts (MIT License)**: All differential equations (`ode.py`), biophysical Hill receptor kinetics (`farmacologia.py`), causal introspection (`pensamento.py`), epistemic surprise (`palavras.py`), RAG gating (`evidence_gate.py`, `contracts/evidence.py`), authorial decoder architectures (`voz.py`, `OwnVoice`), and AST/contract test suites remain permanently open source.
2. **Value Capture via Cloud Pre-Training Infrastructure, Multi-Teacher Distillation, & High-Assurance Epistemic Auditing**: Commercial and institutional adopters pay for **GPU-scale custom pre-training/distillation pipelines**, **domain-verified subquestion adjudication engines**, and **auditable open-system ($O(N)$) cognitive agents** where silent hallucination is unacceptable.
